World Collector — Privacy policy

Effective October 7, 2026.

World Collector recognises what a photo shows and keeps it in a collection. The app is published by the developer of World Collector. This page explains what data leaves your phone, where it goes and why.

What stays on your phone

Your collection, photos and settings are stored on your phone. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not ask for your location. Metadata, including where a photo was taken, is removed from saved photos. You can delete a find in the app; uninstalling the app erases all its data.

Cloud recognition

When a photo is recognised in the cloud, a reduced copy (at most 1024 pixels on its long edge, without metadata) is sent through our Google Apps Script server to the Google Gemini API. We do not keep these photos. Google processes them under the Gemini API terms. With the on-device model switched on in settings, photos are recognised on the phone and are not sent anywhere.

Encyclopedia

To find an article, the app sends the name of the recognised object and the app's language to Wikidata and Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation). Photos are not sent there. Wikimedia handles requests under its privacy policy.

Downloading the on-device model

If you download the on-device model, its files come from Hugging Face, Kaggle and GitHub, which see your IP address as with any download.

Reported chat answers

If you report an answer in a find's chat, we receive your question, the answer, the find's name, the reason, the app's language and version. No photo is sent. Reports are kept for up to 12 months and used only to improve the answers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
